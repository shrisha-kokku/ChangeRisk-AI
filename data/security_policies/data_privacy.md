
# NeoPay Data Privacy Policy

- Personal data (name, email, phone number) must never be written to application logs.
- A user's account deletion request must remove their personal data within 30 days.
- Payment and audit records are the exception: they must be kept for 7 years for
  regulatory reasons, even after account deletion.
- Any deletion of user data must itself be logged, with the requester's ID and timestamp.
